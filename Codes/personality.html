Personality

Three personality traits about myself and how they impact my school life.

Disciplined

After school started I have learnt to ensure that what needs to be done is done. I was never a disciplined person but without adapting this trait my efficiency would not be able to keep up with school. During weekdays I don't allow myself the privilege of social media or playing games because I won't be able to concentrate. This trait allows me to stay on top of my schoolwork and avoid the stress of last-minute submissions and studying.

Accountable

I always have one or two days in the week when my day does not go as intended. When these days happen I must buckle down and ensure that no matter what I reach my daily quota. The day ends at 12pm and if I have 2 hours of work left to do I make sure that I try my best to push myself to finish those 2 hours of work even if it means sleeping a little later. Although it is sometimes stressful, it goes hand in hand with my discipline. It helps me to make sure that I reach my daily quota of work every day.

Persistence

I have a specific routine I follow every day when I can. I have had times where I went three weeks without breaking the routine and following it straight. This persistence allows me to have predictable days and not be bewildered when something out of the ordinary happens. This is because I put my activities in slots in the days where I will most likely have free time and little chances of distraction. This allows me to manage my time properly, do subjects multiple times for the week and cement knowledge

LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch

LLM's have made several advancements over the years that has allowed people to easily create things that otherwise would have taken months or years of learning. Although it sounds like a great tool, they are still able to be wrong. Augenstein et al. (2023) stated that LLMs developed with good intentions may nevertheless be plagued by unreliable information … As a result, chatbots or AI-enhanced search may give inaccurate responses.It encourages students and users to not do their own research because of how confident it can be when giving answers. This can lead to wrong information being taken as accurate and used in graded assignments, study notes for tests and class work.

Additionally, LLM use in assignments can stunt a student's growth in school significantly. These activities, graded or not, are given to help apply the knowledge they have learnt in a way that will help them in the future. Lehmann et al. (2024) found that "when students use LLMs to substitute learning activities, there is a trade-off between volume and understanding. On the one hand, students can increase the volume of topics they cover because they are faster, which increases learning outcomes. On the other hand, this increase in volume comes at the cost of a lesser understanding of each topic, which decreases learning outcomes."

LLM's can be detrimental to a student's studies if used improperly but if used responsibly it can make a student learning experience significantly easier. LLM's should not be used during assignments, instead they should be used before or after assignments for either guidance or an estimate on the correctness of the answers that they provided for each question. This way the student will be able to attempt the assignment by themselves with or without an outline and get instant feedback on what they could potentially do better. The student must note that the LLM is to be used as an assistant not authority. Furthermore, when LLM's are used the information that is given should always be checked for correctness through other sources to minimize hallucinations being taken as correct information.

In conclusion, LLM's are to only be used as a tool due-to its capabilities to provide instant feedback but can reduce overall understanding and provide inaccurate information if used improperly.
